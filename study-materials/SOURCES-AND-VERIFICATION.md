## 2. Key International Standards & RFC Specifications

### IETF Standards (Internet Engineering Task Force)
- **RFC 4944**: *Transmission of IPv6 Packets over IEEE 802.15.4 Networks* (6LoWPAN core specification, encapsulation, and fragmentation).
- **RFC 6282**: *Compression Format for IPv6 Datagrams over IEEE 802.15.4-Based Networks* (IPHC header compression).
- **RFC 7252**: *The Constrained Application Protocol (CoAP)* (Core RESTful UDP standard for constrained devices).
- **RFC 7641**: *Observing Resources in the Constrained Application Protocol (CoAP)* (Asynchronous publish/subscribe extension).
- **RFC 8200**: *Internet Protocol, Version 6 (IPv6) Specification* (Mandating the 1280-byte link MTU).

### IEEE Standards (Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers)
- **IEEE 802.15.4-2015**: *Standard for Low-Rate Wireless Networks (LR-WPAN)* (PHY and MAC layers for 2.4 GHz, 868 MHz, 915 MHz).
- **IEEE 802.11p**: *Wireless Access in Vehicular Environments (WAVE)* (Physical foundation for DSRC in the 5.9 GHz ITS band).
- **IEEE 1609 Family**: *Standards for Dedicated Short Range Communications (DSRC) and Wireless Access in Vehicular Environments*.

### OASIS & ISO Standards
- **ISO/IEC 20922:2016 / OASIS**: *Message Queuing Telemetry Transport (MQTT) Version 3.1.1 Specification*.
- **ISO/IEC 18092 / ISO/IEC 14443**: *Near Field Communication (NFC) Interface and Protocol*.

### NIST Publications (National Institute of Standards and Technology)
- **NIST Special Publication 800-145**: *The NIST Definition of Cloud Computing* (5 characteristics, 3 service models, 4 deployment models).
- **NIST Special Publication 1108R4**: *NIST Framework and Roadmap for Smart Grid Interoperability Standards*.

### Industrial & Telecommunications Standards
- **IEC 62591**: *Industrial communication networks - Wireless communication network and communication profiles - WirelessHART*.
- **ETSI TS 102 690**: *Machine-to-Machine communications (M2M); Functional architecture* (`dIa`, `mId`, `mIa` interfaces).
- **Bluetooth Core Specification Version 5.0**: Bluetooth Special Interest Group (SIG) (BLE GAP/GATT and channel architecture).
