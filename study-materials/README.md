## Welcome & System Overview

This repository contains the authoritative, conceptual textbook for mastering the 12-week NPTEL curriculum on the Internet of Things (IoT). Designed specifically for students preparing for the high-stakes MCQ examination, these materials reject rote memorization in favor of deep architectural intuition, mathematical precision, protocol mechanics, and systematic distractor elimination.

The learning materials are decoupled completely from the interactive question-practice website:
- **`study-materials/` (This Directory)**: Your in-depth textbook and conceptual study center.
- **`mcq-website/`**: Your interactive testing engine featuring the 180 current-session 2026 MCQs and the 724 historical practice questions.

## Core Pedagogical Structure of Each Weekly Lesson

Every single weekly module is structured systematically into the 10-point mastery framework:
1. **Module Header & Lecture Mapping**: Direct cross-reference to Prof. Sudip Misra's slide decks.
2. **Core Concepts (Definition, Purpose & Intuition)**: Clear English definitions paired with vivid real-world analogies.
3. **Step-by-Step Working & Architecture**: Detailed breakdown of operation, handshakes, packet flows, and state machines.
4. **Technical & Mathematical Precision**: Formulas, frequency bands, packet headers, register configs, and timing requirements.
5. **Practical IoT Use Cases**: Grounded applications from smart agriculture, connected healthcare, intelligent grids, and urban telemetry.
6. **Architectural & Comparative Diagrams**: ASCII and Mermaid sequence, topology, and flow diagrams.
7. **Important Vocabulary & Full Forms**: Precise academic definitions tested in NPTEL exams.
8. **Head-to-Head Comparisons**: Direct contrast tables resolving tricky edge cases.
9. **MCQ Traps & Distractor Analysis**: Common pitfalls, misleading answer options, and negative-worded traps.
10. **High-Yield Recall Summary**: Rapid-fire bulleted review points for retention.
